# brief.md — the contract

**This file is the job's contract. The skill refuses to run without it — that's deliberate.**
It lives **inside the folder you're organizing**, not next to the skill. Edit the sentences
to fit your folder; the five decisions underneath them are the part that doesn't change.

---

Use only this folder.

When you're done, I need six folders: Installers, Documents, Images, Archives, Media, and
_Unsorted. Keep the filenames that already make sense. Rename only the meaningless ones. And
leave me an index of every move you made.

Do not delete anything, do not overwrite an original, and do not guess when a file is
ambiguous. If you're not sure, it goes in _Unsorted. And nothing leaves this folder.

Before you change, rename, or move anything, show me the proposed changes and wait.

After I approve the plan, carry it out and return the change list and the files you left
untouched.

---

## What each sentence is doing

The words above can be anything, as long as each sentence still makes its decision:

1. **Bound the input** — what it may use. Nothing outside this folder is its business.
2. **Define finished** — what done looks like. Change the six folders to whatever fits your
   mess; keep "rename only the meaningless ones" unless you want everything renamed.
3. **Protect the boundary** — what must never happen. Delete nothing, guess nothing.
4. **Review before action** — it shows you the plan and waits. Commitment happens after you
   see the proposed change, not before.
5. **Leave a record** — the change list and the index, so "finished" is something you can
   verify instead of something you take on faith.
